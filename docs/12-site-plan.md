## Purpose and approval gate

This document records the current active implementation as a proposed one-page structure. It does not retroactively approve the route, section order, titles, programs, location, calls to action, external destinations, or indexing behavior.

**No new page or route may be built unless it appears here with an `Approved` or `Locked` status and its required business truth and copy are available.** The existence of `site/index.html` is implementation evidence, not client approval.

## Planning rules

- Use a one-page structure when approved, useful content does not justify separate routes.
- Do not create thin service pages merely to increase page or keyword coverage.
- Do not create near-duplicate location pages or substitute town names into repeated copy.
- Do not create a page only for keyword coverage.
- Do not add a route absent from an approved site plan.
- Do not treat the existing build, current website, screenshots, or links as automatic client approval.
- Page purposes, customer questions, primary actions, and business-truth dependencies must be approved before production use.
